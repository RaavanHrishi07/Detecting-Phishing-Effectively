Detecting Phishing Effectively: A Stable and Adaptable Mechanism

Spam detection, supervised learning, regional spam, Monte Carlo approach

In this age of popular instant messaging applications, Short Message Service (SMS) has lost relevance and has turned into the forte of service providers, business houses, and organizations that use this service to target users for marketing and spamming. A recent trend in spam messaging is the use of regional language content typed in English, which makes detection and filtering more challenging. This project uses an extended SMS corpus containing spam and non-spam messages, including those in regional languages like Hindi and Bengali typed in English. Monte Carlo and machine learning algorithms address these challenges effectively.
